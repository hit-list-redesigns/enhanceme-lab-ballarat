Nothing is treated until it’s understood.

We recommend a thorough consultation for every new and existing patient. The clinic’s approach comes down to three plain promises.

Education

As much information about your treatment as we can give you. The team attends regular training to stay current with the latest technologies and products.

Explanation

How a product works, how it’s administered, what to do before and after. We talk it through together, so the plan suits you, not a template.

Consent

Consent forms, a detailed health history and a full rundown of the risks. Treatment proceeds only once you’re assessed as a good candidate, under AHPRA’s non-surgical cosmetic guidelines.

How a visit runs

The order matters, so here it is.

  1. IConsult15 minSit down with an experienced injector to talk through your concerns and the outcome you want, with no treatment on the day.
  2. IITreat45 minReady to go ahead? A new-patient appointment with our RN, Yasmin, leaves time to consult and treat on the same day.
  3. IIIAftercareAt homeWritten aftercare guides, from microneedling to laser.
  4. IVReviewFree · 15 minCome back 2 to 6 weeks after a procedure to check results and ask anything.
